Delete Your ProfitX Account

This page provides an external way to request deletion of your ProfitX account and associated personal data.

Delete from inside the app

If you can access your account, use the Delete Account option inside ProfitX. The app will ask you to confirm before permanently deleting the account.

Request deletion without the app

If you cannot access ProfitX, send a deletion request from the email address associated with your ProfitX account.

  1. Click the button below.
  2. Send the email from the address registered with ProfitX.
  3. Include your ProfitX username if you know it.
  4. We may reply to verify account ownership before deletion.
Security: Do not send your ProfitX password or any financial credentials in your deletion request.
Send Account Deletion Request

What is deleted

When an account deletion request is completed, the ProfitX account and personal data associated with that account are deleted, subject to limited retention where required for security, fraud prevention, legal compliance, dispute resolution, or service-provider backup processes.
